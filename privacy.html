Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 28, 2026

This application is used solely for personal academic research to access and analyze data from an Oura Ring account authorized by the user.

Data accessed

With the user’s permission, the app may access sleep and related physiological data (e.g., heart rate and HRV) made available via the Oura API.

How data is used

  • To download and analyze the authorized user’s own data for non-commercial research and visualization.
  • Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Data sharing

The app does not share data with third parties. Data may be stored locally by the user for analysis.

Data retention

Data retention depends on the user’s local storage and research workflow. The developer does not operate a hosted database for this application.

Security

Access tokens and downloaded data should be treated as sensitive and stored securely by the user.
